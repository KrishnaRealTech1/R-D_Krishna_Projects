PowerDetect (Auto/Manual select): RA0 (digital input)

Trigger (Auto mode command): RA1 (digital input)

ManualTrigger: RA2 (digital input)

LimitSwitch pulses: RB0/INT0 (digital input with INT0 edge detect)

OpenFeedback: RA3 (digital input)

CloseFeedback: RA4 (digital input)

OpenRelay: RB1 (output)

CloseRelay: RB2 (output)

OpenStatus: RB3 (output)

CloseStatus: RB4 (output)

LED_HEARTBEAT: RB5 (output) — simple alive indicator

I²C: SCL RC3, SDA RC4

UART: TX RC6, RX RC7 (HC-05)





Inputs – 6 lines
RA0 – PowerDetect (Auto/Manual select)

RA1 – Trigger (Auto mode command)

RA2 – ManualTrigger (gate for manual control)

RA3 – OpenFeedback (manual mode → close)

RA4 – CloseFeedback (manual mode → open)

RB0 – LimitSwitch (pulses, INT0)

Outputs – 5 lines
RB1 – OpenRelay control

RB2 – CloseRelay control

RB3 – OpenStatus output

RB4 – CloseStatus output

RB5 – Heartbeat LED

Special-function I/O (peripherals) – 2 lines
RC3 – I²C SCL (OLED)

RC4 – I²C SDA (OLED)

Extra (not counted as new GPIO)
RC6 (TX) / RC7 (RX) – UART for HC-05 Bluetooth module.




Pin 1 MCLR/VPP 			 → 10 kΩ ↑ to +5 V (Reset/ICSP)

Pin 2 RA0 				 → PowerDetect (10 kΩ ↓ to GND)

Pin 3 RA1				 → Trigger (10 kΩ ↓)

Pin 4 RA2				 → ManualTrigger (10 kΩ ↓)

Pin 5 RA3				 → OpenFeedback (10 kΩ ↓)

Pin 6 RA4				 → CloseFeedback (10 kΩ ↓)

Pin 8 VSS 				 → GND

Pin 14 RC3 				 → SCL (OLED, pull-up)

Pin 15 RC4 				 → SDA (OLED, pull-up)

Pin 17 RC6				 → TX → HC-05 RXD (via divider)

Pin 18 RC7				 ← RX ← HC-05 TXD

Pin 19 VSS 				 → GND

Pin 20 VDD				 → +5 V

Pin 21 RB0/INT0			 ← Limit Switch (10 kΩ ↓; optional RC)

Pin 22 RB1 				 → OpenRelay (BC547 + flyback)

Pin 23 RB2 				 → CloseRelay (BC547 + flyback)

Pin 24 RB3				 → OpenStatus out

Pin 25 RB4 				 → CloseStatus out

Pin 26 RB5				 → Heartbeat LED (optional)
