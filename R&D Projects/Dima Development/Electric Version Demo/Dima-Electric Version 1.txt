Want to develop the Hardware and software for the Vaccum Cleaner.


Materials:
1). Brush Motor Relay.
2). Vaccum Motor Relay.
3). Handel Switch 1.
4). Handel Switch 2.
5). Liver Switch.
6). PIC12F683.

I/Os:
Input  - 3.
Output - 2.


Operations:
1). Handel Switch 1 And Handel Switch 2 is HIGH. The Brush Motor will Turn ON.
2). Liver Switch is High. Turn ON the Vaccum Motor.
3). Liver Switch is LOW. Turn OFF the Vaccum Motor after 10secs.
4). Handel Switch 1 And Handel Switch 2 is Low. The Brush Motor will Turn OFF.
5). If Handel Switch 1 or 2 low for 5Mins, Turn OFF the Brush Motor. Again Handel Switch 1 And Handel Switch 2 is High, Turn ON the Brush Motor.






| PIC Pin | Name     | Dir | Function                                  |
| ------- | -------- | --: | ----------------------------------------- |
| 1       | VDD      |   — | +5 V                                      |
| 2       | GP5      | Out | **BRUSH_RELAY** (to BC547 base via 1 kΩ)  |
| 3       | GP4      |  In | **HANDLE_SW2**                            |
| 4       | GP3/MCLR |   — | Keep as MCLR/VPP (not used as I/O)        |
| 5       | GP2      |  In | **LEVER_SW**                              |
| 6       | GP1      |  In | **HANDLE_SW1**                            |
| 7       | GP0      | Out | **VACUUM_RELAY** (to BC547 base via 1 kΩ) |
| 8       | VSS      |   — | GND                                       |




GP1 (pin 6) ← Handle Switch 1 — 10 kΩ to GND

GP4 (pin 3) ← Handle Switch 2 — 10 kΩ to GND

GP2 (pin 5) ← Lever Switch — 10 kΩ to GND


PIC output → 1 kΩ → BC547 base

BC547 emitter → GND

Relay coil: one side to +Vcoil (5 V or 12 V), other side to BC547 collector

Flyback diode across coil: 1N4148/1N4007, cathode → +Vcoil, anode → collector

Base-emitter resistor: 47 kΩ – 100 kΩ from base to emitter (keeps transistor OFF at reset)

If switching an AC motor with relay contacts, add RC snubber across contacts: ~100 Ω + 100 nF (X2 cap) in series.

Capacitor: 100 nF (0.1 µF), X2 safety class, rated ≥ 275 VAC (many are 305 VAC).

Resistor: 100 Ω, 0.5 W or 1 W, flame-proof metal film (surge-tolerant).
