Project - Dima
Version - Battery Version - 1



Want to develop the Hardware and software for the Vaccum Cleaner.


Materials:
1). Brush Motor Relay.
2). Vaccum Motor Relay.
3). Handel Switch 1.
4). Handel Switch 2.
5). Liver Switch.
6). OLED Display.
7). PIC18F46K42.
8). RTC Module.



Operations:
1). Handel Switch 1 And Handel Switch 2 is HIGH. The Brush Motor will Turn ON.
2). Liver Switch is High. Turn ON the Vaccum Motor.
3). Liver Switch is LOW. Turn OFF the Vaccum Motor after 10secs.
4). Handel Switch 1 And Handel Switch 2 is Low. The Brush Motor will Turn OFF.
5). If Handel Switch 1 or 2 low for 5Mins, Turn OFF the Brush Motor. Again Handel Switch 1 And Handel Switch 2 is High, Turn ON the Brush Motor.
6). Current Sensing Circuit is used to measure the DC Motor current and also make cutoff when high current taken by DC Motor.
7). If the Current(Amps) is Cross the setting Amps, Turn OFF the Motor.


Display Interface:
1). Display Should Show Brush Motor Running Time(How many mins it was Run).
2). Display Should Show Vaccum Motor Running Time(How many mins it was Run).
3). Overall MAchine Running Time (How many mins it was Run).
4). Display Should Shows Battery level(Symbol Indication) with Battery Voltage.
5). Display Should Shows Brush Motor Current(Amps) and Vaccum Motor Current(Amps).
