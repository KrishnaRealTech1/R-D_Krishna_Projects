Pin mapping (final)
Power, clock, debug (keep as-is)

VDD, VDDA → 3.3 V (decouple per ST: 0.1 µF at each VDD, + 4.7–10 µF bulk) 
STMicroelectronics

VSS, VSSA → GND

NRST → Reset button + pull-up

SWDIO / SWCLK → PA13 / PA14 (ST-Link)

BOOT0 → Pull-down to GND (jumper if you want bootloader)

I²C bus (LCD 16×2 via PCF8574 + RTC)

PB8 – I2C1_SCL

PB9 – I2C1_SDA
Pull-ups: 4.7 kΩ to 3.3 V (one pair shared for both devices). 
STMicroelectronics
DigiKey

UARTs

Bluetooth (HC-05, 3.3 V logic) – USART1

PA9 – USART1_TX → HC-05 RXD

PA10 – USART1_RX → HC-05 TXD

Weight Scale RS-232 – USART2 via MAX3232

PA2 – USART2_TX → MAX3232 T1IN → Scale RX

PA3 – USART2_RX ← MAX3232 R1OUT ← Scale TX
(MAX3232 VCC=3.3 V, C1..C4 charge-pump caps per ref design.)

GSM module – USART3

PB10 – USART3_TX → GSM RX

PB11 – USART3_RX ← GSM TX
(Most GSM UARTs are 2.8–3.8 V tolerant—confirm your module’s IO level; level shift if needed.) 
STMicroelectronics

GSM control

PC13 – GSM_GKEY (GPIO output) → NPN/MOSFET level driver if module pin isn’t strictly 3.3 V tolerant. Your sequence: 3.3 V for 3 s after boot, then low.

Digital inputs (float switches, active-closed to GND recommended)

PB1 – FLOAT_TOP (EXTI capable) with internal pull-up (or external 10 kΩ to 3.3 V)

PB2 – FLOAT_BOTTOM (EXTI capable) with internal pull-up

High-side loads (motors / solenoids / pump) — low-side N-MOSFET drive pins

(Use logic-level MOSFETs + flyback diodes. If coils are 12 V, supply from 12 V; gate at 3.3 V is fine with suitable FET.)

PA0 – DOSING_PUMP (TIM2_CH1 PWM-capable if you ever need speed control)

PA1 – VIBRATOR (TIM2_CH2)

PA4 – MIXER_MOTOR (SPI1_NSS also; OK as GPIO)

PA5 – WATER_IN_SOL

PA6 – WATER_OUT_SOL

PA7 – DRAIN_OUT_SOL

PA3 – ROTATE_MOTOR (TIM2_CH4 alt; here as GPIO)

PB0 – BUBBLER_MOTOR (TIM3_CH3)
